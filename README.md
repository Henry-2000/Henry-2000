### About

PhD student in the Graduate Program in Electronic Engineering and Computing (Telecommunications area) at **ITA — Instituto Tecnológico de Aeronáutica**, São José dos Campos, Brazil. Advised by Prof. Sarah Negreiros de Carvalho Leite.

My research is in **NILM (Non-Intrusive Load Monitoring)** — disaggregating a household's total electricity signal into individual appliance loads using machine learning — with a long-term goal of embedded deployment in smart meters.

- 🎓 PhD started February 2026 · expected completion ~Dec 2029 / Feb 2030
- 🔬 Background in stochastic processes and statistical signal processing (WSS processes, PSD, blind source separation)
- 🧠 Applies deep learning architectures to NILM disaggregation
- 📍 São José dos Campos, Brazil

### Research trajectory

| Stage | Approach | Status |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Seq2Seq RNNs (LSTM, GRU, BiLSTM, BiGRU, SimpleRNN) with sparsity-weighted adaptive loss and a gated decoder | Master's thesis, REDD dataset — manuscript under review, IEEE Transactions journal |
| 2 | Transformer-based disaggregation — shared encoder with appliance-specific cross-attention decoder heads | Current PhD work, REDD dataset — IEEE-format paper drafted |
| 3 | Probabilistic unsupervised adaptation (F-HDPHSMM) | In progress |

Evaluated with F1-score, MAE, and Signal Aggregate Error (SAE). Also exploring zero-/few-shot LLM-based disaggregation as a side track.
